Nikita Kalinin

Institute of Science and Technology Austria.

I am a PhD student at the Institute of Science and Technology Austria (ISTA), advised by Christoph Lampert.

My research focuses on the theoretical foundations of differential privacy, particularly matrix factorization mechanisms for private machine learning and continual observation. I enjoy designing algorithms and studying their theoretical properties. More broadly, I am interested in connections between privacy, algorithms, statistics, learning theory, and optimization.
